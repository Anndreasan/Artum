ABOUT THE STUDIO

Making things by hand, with curiosity.

ARTUM is an independent creative studio exploring furniture, objects, art and everyday materials.

The studio is built around a simple idea: make interesting things, learn by doing and let materials guide the process. Some projects become products. Others stay experiments.

Based in Stockholm, Sweden 2026.



ABOUT ME

From a young age, I’ve enjoyed working with wood, metal, and different materials to bring my own ideas and solutions to life. I’ve tried a bit of everything — from making rings out of nuts in my kitchen and throwing paint onto canvases in my apartment, to creating larger wall paintings to sell, tattooing at parties, and even cutting into my living room floor to build a coffee table. There have been many different projects along the way, often starting as nothing more than an idea in my head and gradually turning into something physical.

I don’t believe everything has to be perfect. What matters to me is the process of taking something that exists in my imagination and giving it a physical form, whether it becomes a finished product, an experiment, or simply an idea that leads to the next one. For me, creativity is about curiosity, getting hands-on, seeing possibilities, and turning something that only existed in my head into something real.
